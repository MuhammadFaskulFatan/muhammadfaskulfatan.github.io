latest project

Did the online shopping boom after 2020 last?

I went through the real purchase histories of 5,014 US Amazon accounts, 2018 to 2022 (shared openly by researchers at MIT), to see what actually stuck after the 2020 boom.

+60%more spend per customer in 2022 than in 2019, after inflation
2 in 3customers kept their 2020 spending habits
8 in 10parts of the growth came from shopping more often, not bigger orders
